## Delivered behavior

[Methods](PRACTICAL_PLANS_METHODS.md) describe the algorithm and assumptions;
[usage](PRACTICAL_PLANS_USAGE.md) gives the ordinary controls.

`practical_glassing/timed.py` retains sparse **unselected** cell/patch support,
uses actual scheduled stop minutes, and calculates exact cellwise-max unique
opportunity. A separate optional per-stop cap has no static-30-minute or 120-minute
clamp. Independent exact knapsacks are a lower bound; both visit-start orders and
two coordinate improvement sweeps select patches jointly. Full patch costs remain
fixed. There is no global-optimum or calibrated scan-rate claim.

`selection.py` supplies exhaustive singles/both pair orders within the existing
8–80 proposal cap and separate AM/PM frontiers/recommendations. Practical selection
influences the pool, alternatives, frontiers, recommendation and contribution units.
The old positive-v1 admission and top-eight pair screen do not screen practical
options. Actual connected outbound/transition/return geometry, directional effort,
pins, restrictions and independent reserve limits are checked. Existing sampling
and coarse observer screens remain; they can miss useful openings.

`replan.py` reuses sealed route evidence for schedule/policy edits. Profile, date,
entry, access and geometry changes miss that cache. `cache.py` marks complete
publication atomically; interrupted generations are retained and recomputed, while
corrupt completed caches fail visibly. Sparse support is capped at 256 MiB, analysis
windows remain bounded, and no whole-unit candidate/scenario cube is created.

`inspection.py` supplies a 150 m local context and coarse risk along selected
azimuths in 10–50/50–150/150–500 m bands, for at most eight distinct finalists.
No compatible measured foreground input or matching perspective scene was validated
for these finalists. The existing scene metadata search found no scene within 2 km.
RCMAP cover is not horizontal transmission. Low cover does not establish a clear
opening; high cover does not prove blockage. Inspection never changes scores.
Measured numerical obstruction, a new measured-data fixture/integration path, new
imagery acquisition and field-observation collection are **deferred**, not passed
acceptance. Existing owner inspection/scene systems remain untouched.

The browser displays actual selected support separately from theoretical terrain
and full scan footprints. It exposes stop minutes, unused/rest time, habitat/range
contributions and foreground uncertainty. A reproduced startup race was fixed:
delayed saved-analysis hydration cannot overwrite newer user edits. Version-2 saves
retain explicit grid/source/mask/profile/route/schedule/scan identities; original
version-1 saves remain readable/exportable with original scores. JSON, summaries,
GeoJSON sectors and ordered GPX route/return exports agree. Static five-point reviews
retain their distinct waypoint-only GPX and correct non-itinerary label. Combined/
stay days are **established-mode-only**; no practical score is attached to them.
